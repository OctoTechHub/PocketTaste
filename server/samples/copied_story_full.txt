Raat ke gyarah baj kar chhabbis minute. Rahul apne kamre mein akela tha, laptop band kar chuka tha, aur bas sone hi wala tha ki uska phone vibrate hua.

Ek unknown number se message aata hai: 'main tumhare theek peeche khada hoon.' Rahul peeche mudta hai aur usi pal phone ki battery 0% ho jaati hai.

Kamre mein andhera tha. Rahul ne apne haath se deewar tatolte hue switch dhoondha. Light jali. Peeche koi nahin tha. Sirf uski almari, ek khuli khidki, aur pankhe ki halki si awaaz.

Usne socha koi mazaak kar raha hoga. Office ke ladke aksar aise number se message bhejte the. Phone charge par lagaya. Screen jali to teen aur message aa chuke the, usi number se.

'Tumne light kyun jalayi.'
'Ab main almari ke andar hoon.'
'Ginti shuru karo. Teen.'

Rahul ke haath thande pad gaye. Usne almari ki taraf dekha. Uska darwaza pehle band tha. Ab woh do inch khula hua tha.

'Do.'

Usne phone uthaya aur police ka number dabaya. Ghanti gayi, lekin doosri taraf se koi awaaz nahin aayi. Sirf saans. Bahut kareeb se aati hui saans, jaise koi phone ke doosri taraf nahin, balki kamre ke andar khada ho.

'Ek.'

Almari ka darwaza poora khul gaya.

Andar kuch nahin tha. Sirf uske purane kapde, ek suitcase, aur deewar par ek chhota sa aaina jo pehle kabhi wahan nahin tha. Rahul ne aaine mein dekha. Usme uska chehra tha, lekin woh muskura raha tha. Rahul muskura nahin raha tha.

Agli subah Rahul ki maa ne uska darwaza khatkhataya. Koi jawab nahin aaya. Darwaza andar se band tha. Chaabi se khola gaya to kamra khaali tha. Khidki band thi. Phone charging par laga hua tha, poora charge.

Uske phone mein ek hi message tha, jo usi raat gyarah baj kar chhabbis minute par bheja gaya tha. Number Rahul ka apna tha.

'Main tumhare theek peeche khada hoon.'

Police ne case teen mahine baad band kar diya. Kaha gaya ki ladka ghar chhod kar chala gaya hoga. Lekin us kamre mein ab bhi koi nahin sota. Aur har raat gyarah baj kar chhabbis minute par, us ghar ke har phone ki battery ek saath 0% ho jaati hai.
