TITLE: Namak Ka Bahi-Khata
LANGUAGE: hi
GENRES: thriller
DURATION_SECONDS: 20000

DESCRIPTION:
1890 ke Bombay mein ek andha munshi namak ke vyapar ka hisaab rakhta hai. Ek din use
ek purana khata milta hai jiske panne khaali nahin hain.

STORY:
Vithal ki aankhein bachpan mein chali gayi thin, par ungliyan syahi ki umar pehchan
leti hain. Namak ke godaam ka har hisaab uske dimaag mein rehta hai. Us subah use jo
khata mila, usme un logon ke karz likhe the jo abhi paida hi nahin hue. Har naam ke
saamne ek tareekh thi, aur unme se ek tareekh kal ki thi. Jis inspector ne use kaam
par rakha tha, woh register ke mutabik do saal pehle mar chuka hai, lekin har subah
wahi aadmi aakar haazri lagata hai.
